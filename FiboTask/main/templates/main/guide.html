Use FiboTask in three loops

Size the work, split the scary parts, start with a tiny timer. Everything below maps to a button you will actually see.

1. Estimate with points, not hours

Pick a baseline you know well. Example: reviewing 5 flashcards is your 2. New work gets sized relative to it: easier is a 1, a bit harder is a 3 or 5, unknown steps jump to 8. Hours lie, comparisons do not.

Where to tap: Tasks page, open Course, priority, points, due date, then the points dropdown. Or Command, Estimator.

2. Split anything 8 or higher

Rule of 8: an 8 never sits on today's list. Open its Steps box and add the first 2 minute move (open the doc, write the title). The app nudges you until steps exist.

Where to tap: each task, Steps, then Add step. Tick steps off as you focus.

3. Plan a sprint you can finish

Tap the bolt icon on a few tasks, or Auto-plan to fill by priority inside your capacity (default 21 pts, about one big week). Over capacity? Drop something or split a 13.

Where to tap: Tasks sidebar, Sprint card. Filter with the Sprint tab to see only this week's work.

4. Start with the 1 to 13 ladder

Procrastinating? Do not "start the essay". Start 1 minute: clear desk. Then 2: open tools. 3: outline. 5: ugly draft. Momentum carries you into 13, 21 or 34 minute deep work.

Why 13? 1, 2, 3 and 5 add up to 11 minutes of warm-up. 13 is the first rung that needs real focus: short enough to start, long enough to matter. It pairs with a 5 minute break.

FAQ

Why do the gaps widen (5 to 8 to 13)?

Because big work is uncertain. Forcing a choice between 8 and 13 surfaces hidden complexity that 8, 9, 10 would let you ignore.

Why 13 minutes and not 25?

25 minute Pomodoros fail at the starting line for procrastinators. 13 follows an 11 minute warm-up ladder, so resistance is already gone when deep work begins. Graduate to 21 and 34 when 13 feels easy.

What if everything feels like a 13?

Your baseline is off. Pick a smaller 2 you do weekly, re-size everything against it, and split the 13s before they touch today.

Can I change sprint capacity?

Yes, in the Sprint card dropdown (1 to 21). Start at 21, lower it if you keep overflowing.

New here? Don't start blank.

On an empty list, press Load 3 sample tasks: a 5-pointer with steps, a 3, and a 1-point warm-up. Delete them whenever. Your data is yours: Export tasks (CSV) sits under the Today card.

Do I need an account?

No. This build keeps everything local-first so you can start in seconds.
